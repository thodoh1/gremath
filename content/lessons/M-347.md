# Lesson M-347 — Local extrema

Part H. H. Multivariable Calculus · **Optimization** · High

This lesson isolates **Local extrema** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a High skill: it is standard GRE material. You should be able to do it without warming up. Multivariable calculus is explicitly in the calculus portion: partials, gradients, multiple integrals, Jacobians.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Local extrema**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

Local extrema are neighborhood min/max. A saddle is a critical point that is neither. In higher dimensions: if $H$ is positive definite, local min; negative definite, local max; indefinite, saddle. Semidefinite: inconclusive.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** $f=(y-x^2)(y-2x^2)$ at the origin: a famous monkey saddle / degenerate example (awareness).

**Solution.** The origin can fail to be a local min along some parabolas and be a min along axes — test inconclusive from Hessian $0$.

## 3. Precise statement

Maximum likelihood: given iid density/pmf $f(x\mid\theta)$, the likelihood is $L(\theta)=\prod_{i=1}^n f(X_i\mid\theta)$. An MLE $\hat\theta$ maximizes $L$ (equivalently $\ell=\log L$) over the parameter space. Interior MLEs often solve $\ell'(\theta)=0$. Invariance: the MLE of $g(\theta)$ is $g(\hat\theta)$.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** iid Bernoulli($p$): MLE of $p$.

**Solution.** $\ell=(\sum x_i)\log p+(n-\sum x_i)\log(1-p)$, so $\hat p=\bar X$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Maximizing $L$ in $x$ instead of $\theta$, or ignoring the constraint (e.g. $\mathrm{Unif}[0,\theta]$ MLE is $X_{(n)}$, not a critical point of a differentiable $L$).
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

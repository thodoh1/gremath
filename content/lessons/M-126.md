# Lesson M-126 — Signs in quadrants

Part C. C. Trigonometry & Conics · **Trigonometry foundations** · Critical

Prerequisites: `B.1`.


This lesson isolates **Signs in quadrants** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Critical skill: it is used constantly, and a hole here leaks points all over the paper. Trigonometry appears in calculus, geometry, and as standalone identity/equation items. Exact values and identities are speed.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Signs in quadrants**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 2. From zero

### Landscape of *Trigonometry foundations*

Radians are the calculus unit. The unit circle defines $\sin,\cos$ as coordinates, and the other four functions as ratios. Pythagorean, even/odd, and reciprocal identities are rewriting tools. Exact values at $0,\pi/6,\pi/4,\pi/3,\pi/2$ and their cousins must be instant. Quadrant signs are ASTC (or whatever mnemonic you will not botch).

### This skill, specifically

Radians are the calculus unit.

You are not learning the whole topic again. You are learning the exact move named in the title. Neighboring lessons exist; use them if a word here is still foreign, then come back.

## 3. Precise statement

Signs: Q1 all $+$; Q2 $\sin,\csc+$ ; Q3 $\tan,\cot+$ ; Q4 $\cos,\sec+$. ASTC (or CAST, depending on where you start).

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

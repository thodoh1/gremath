# Lesson M-873 — Standard deviation

Part U. U. Mathematical Statistics · **Descriptive statistics** · Medium

Prerequisites: `T.2`.


This lesson isolates **Standard deviation** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Statistics is lighter than probability but bias, MLE, and tests do appear.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Standard deviation**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

Standard deviation is $\sigma=\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(X)}$ (same units as $X$). Sample sd is $s=\sqrt{s^2}$. Scaling: $\mathrm{sd}(aX+b)=|a|\,\mathrm{sd}(X)$. Chebyshev: $P(|X-\mu|\ge k\sigma)\le 1/k^2$ for $k>0$, with no extra distributional hypothesis.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** Chebyshev bound on $P(|X-\mu|\ge 2\sigma)$.

**Solution.** $\le 1/4$.

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** Sd of a Bernoulli($p$) random variable.

**Solution.** $\sqrt{p(1-p)}$.

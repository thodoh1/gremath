# Lesson M-778 — Linear recurrences

Part R. R. Combinatorics · **Advanced counting tools** · Medium

Prerequisites: `R.2-G.2`.


This lesson isolates **Linear recurrences** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Combinatorics is additional topics and also probability setup.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Linear recurrences**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

A linear homogeneous recurrence $a_n=c_1 a_{n-1}+\cdots+c_d a_{n-d}$ is determined by $d$ consecutive initial values. First-order $a_n=r a_{n-1}$ solves as $a_n=a_0 r^n$. Nonhomogeneous equations add a particular solution of the same shape as the forcing term.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** $a_n=4a_{n-1}$ with $a_0=7$. Find $a_n$ and $a_3$.

**Solution.** $a_n=7\cdot 4^n$, $a_3=448$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** $a_n=a_{n-1}+6$ with $a_0=2$. Closed form?

**Solution.** $a_n=2+6n$.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Solving $a_n=3a_{n-1}+2$ as if it were homogeneous, getting only $A\cdot 3^n$ and missing the particular constant $-1$.
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** $a_n=3a_{n-1}+2$, $a_0=1$. Find $a_n$.

**Solution.** Particular $a=-1$; homogeneous $A\cdot 3^n$; $A-1=1$ so $A=2$; $a_n=2\cdot 3^n-1$. Check $a_1=5=3\cdot 1+2$.

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** Fibonacci $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$, $F_n=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}$. $F_8$.

**Solution.** $21$. Sequence: $0,1,1,2,3,5,8,13,21$.

## 9. Lock it in

You own this skill when you can do Drill 1 and Drill 2 tomorrow, mixed among unrelated problems, in under five minutes each, with the traps named in Section 7 avoided on purpose.

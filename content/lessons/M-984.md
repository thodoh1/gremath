# Lesson M-984 — Simpson rule

Part X. X. Numerical Analysis · **Numerical integration & ODEs** · Medium

Prerequisites: `F.2-J.1`.


This lesson isolates **Simpson rule** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Numerical methods are additional topics: Newton, error, quadrature recognition.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Simpson rule**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 2. From zero

### Landscape of *Numerical integration & ODEs*

### This skill, specifically

Trapezoid, Simpson, error orders, Euler $y_{n+1}=y_n+hf(y_n)$, Runge–Kutta as higher-order idea, stability of stepping.

You are not learning the whole topic again. You are learning the exact move named in the title. Neighboring lessons exist; use them if a word here is still foreign, then come back.

## 3. Precise statement

Simpson’s rule (composite, $n$ even): $S=\dfrac{h}{3}\bigl(y_0+4y_1+2y_2+4y_3+\cdots+4y_{n-1}+y_n\bigr)$ with $h=(b-a)/n$. Exact for degree $\le 3$ (not merely $2$). Error $-\dfrac{(b-a)h^4}{180}f^{(4)}(\xi)$ for $f\in C^4$. One panel Simpson is the interpolatory integral of a quadratic through the two ends and the midpoint.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Simpson $n=2$ for $\int_0^1 x^3\,dx$.

**Solution.** $h=1/2$, $S=\frac{1/2}{3}\bigl(0+4\cdot(1/2)^3+1\bigr)=\frac16(0+4\cdot 1/8+1)=\frac16\cdot\frac32=1/4$, exact.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** Pattern of interior weights (after factor $h/3$):

**Solution.** $4,2,4,2,\ldots,4$.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Using Simpson with an odd number of panels, or the factor $h/2$ from trapezoid.
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** Exactness degree of Simpson.

**Solution.** $3$.

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** One-panel Simpson for $\int_{-1}^1 1\,dx$.

**Solution.** $2$, exact.

# Lesson M-987 — Runge-Kutta method awareness

Part X. X. Numerical Analysis · **Numerical integration & ODEs** · Medium

Prerequisites: `F.2-J.1`.


This lesson isolates **Runge-Kutta method awareness** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Numerical methods are additional topics: Newton, error, quadrature recognition.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Runge-Kutta method awareness**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

A Runge–Kutta method stages several Euler-like evaluations of $f$ inside a step and combines them. Classic RK4 uses four evaluations: $k_1=f(t,y)$, $k_2=f(t+h/2,y+hk_1/2)$, $k_3=f(t+h/2,y+hk_2/2)$, $k_4=f(t+h,y+hk_3)$, then $y_{+}=y+(h/6)(k_1+2k_2+2k_3+k_4)$. Order $4$ for smooth $f$. RK2 (Heun/midpoint) is the order-$2$ cousin. GRE: recognize the pattern and the order, not memorize every tableau.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Heun (improved Euler) for $y'=f(t,y)$: predictor $\tilde y=y+h f(t,y)$, then $y_{+}=y+\frac h2\bigl(f(t,y)+f(t+h,\tilde y)\bigr)$. One step on $y'=y$, $y(0)=1$, $h=1$.

**Solution.** $\tilde y=2$, $y_{+}=1+\frac12(1+2)=2.5$, closer to $e$ than Euler’s $2$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** RK4 uses how many $f$-evaluations per step?

**Solution.** $4$.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Calling any multi-stage method ‘RK4’, or confusing local order with the number of stages in general (they need not match).
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** Midpoint RK2: $k_1=f(t,y)$, $k_2=f(t+h/2,y+hk_1/2)$, $y_{+}=y+h k_2$. Order?

**Solution.** $2$.

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** If $f$ is independent of $y$ and linear in $t$, RK4 on one step reproduces

**Solution.** Simpson for the integral $y(t+h)-y(t)=\int_t^{t+h} f(s)\,ds$.

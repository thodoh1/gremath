# Lesson M-875 — Correlation

Part U. U. Mathematical Statistics · **Descriptive statistics** · Medium

This lesson isolates **Correlation** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Statistics is lighter than probability but bias, MLE, and tests do appear.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Correlation**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

Pearson correlation $\rho(X,Y)=\dfrac{\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)}{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}$ for $\sigma_X\sigma_Y\neq 0$. Always $\rho\in[-1,1]$, with $|\rho|=1$ iff $Y=aX+b$ a.s. with $a\neq 0$ (sign of $a$ equals sign of $\rho$). $\rho$ is invariant under positive affine rescaling of either variable.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

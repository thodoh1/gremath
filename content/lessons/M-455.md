# Lesson M-455 — Row space

Part K. K. Linear Algebra · **Vector spaces** · Critical

Prerequisites: `K.1-K.3`.


This lesson isolates **Row space** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Critical skill: it is used constantly, and a hole here leaks points all over the paper. Linear algebra is a large share of the algebra portion. Rank, kernels, eigenvalues, and determinants are exam staples.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Row space**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 2. From zero

### Landscape of *Vector spaces*

Axioms, subspaces, span, independence, basis, dimension, coordinates, the four fundamental subspaces, rank-nullity. This is the language that makes matrices mean something.

### This skill, specifically

Axioms, subspaces, span, independence, basis, dimension, coordinates, the four fundamental subspaces, rank-nullity.

You are not learning the whole topic again. You are learning the exact move named in the title. Neighboring lessons exist; use them if a word here is still foreign, then come back.

## 3. Precise statement

The row space $\mathrm{Row}\,A$ is the span of the rows, a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$ for $A$ $m\times n$. Row ops do not change $\mathrm{Row}\,A$, so the nonzero rows of REF form a basis. $\dim\mathrm{Row}\,A=\dim\mathrm{Col}\,A=\mathrm{rank}\,A$. $\mathrm{Row}\,A=\mathrm{Col}\,A^T$.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** A basis of $\mathrm{Row}\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3\\2&4&6\\1&0&1\end{pmatrix}$.

**Solution.** $R_2-2R_1=\mathbf{0}$, $R_3-R_1=(0,-2,-2)$. Nonzero REF rows $(1,2,3)$, $(0,1,1)$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** $\dim\mathrm{Row}\,A$ for rank $2$ is

**Solution.** $2$.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Using original rows that are dependent as a 'basis' without discarding, or claiming row space lives in $\mathbb{R}^m$.
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** $\mathrm{Row}\,A$ for $A$ of size $3\times 5$ is a subspace of

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** Row ops change $\mathrm{Col}\,A$ but not

**Solution.** $\mathrm{Row}\,A$ (nor $\mathrm{Nul}\,A$).

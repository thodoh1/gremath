## 3. Precise statement

A chord is a segment joining two points of a circle. The perpendicular from the center to a chord bisects the chord, and the converse holds. Equal chords are equidistant from the center and subtend equal central angles. Power: if two chords $AB,CD$ meet at $P$ (inside), then $AP\cdot PB=CP\cdot PD$.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Radius $13$, chord of length $10$. Distance from center to the chord?

**Solution.** Half-chord $5$, so $d=\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=12$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

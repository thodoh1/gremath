This lesson isolates **All Critical items reached Level 3 mastery** and trains it until it is automatic.

## 1. Why this skill exists

## 3. Precise statement

Gate — Critical skills at Level $3$: for each Critical skill, produce $4/4$ consecutive mixed items with all of (i) named governing theorem or definition, (ii) hypotheses checked or a counterexample if they fail, (iii) a correct computation or classification, (iv) the trap named. A Level-$2$ ‘I recognize it with a hint’ does not pass. The diagnostic below is the template; apply it to every Critical item in Parts A–Y.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Diagnostic four-pack (must be $4/4$): (1) Rolle on $f(x)=x^2-1$ on $[-1,1]$: find $c$. (2) Bayes: $P(D)=0.01$, $P(+|D)=0.99$, $P(+|D^c)=0.01$, find $P(D|+)$. (3) CR: is $\overline{z}$ holomorphic? (4) Rank-nullity for a $3\times 4$ matrix of rank $2$: nullity?

**Solution.** (1) $c=0$. (2) $P(D|+)=0.99\cdot 0.01/(0.99\cdot 0.01+0.01\cdot 0.99)=1/2$. (3) No, CR fails. (4) Nullity $2$. Pass only if all four include hypothesis/trap notes (Rolle needs $f(\pm 1)$ equal; Bayes needs the law of total probability; CR is necessary; rank-nullity needs finite dimensions).

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

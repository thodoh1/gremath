This lesson isolates **Covariance/correlation relationship** and trains it until it is automatic.

## 3. Precise statement

Sample covariance $s_{xy}=\dfrac{1}{n-1}\sum(x_i-\bar x)(y_i-\bar y)$ and sample correlation $\hat\rho=s_{xy}/(s_x s_y)$. The LS slope is $\hat\beta_1=s_{xy}/s_x^2=\hat\rho\, s_y/s_x$. Thus $\hat\beta_1$ and $\hat\rho$ share sign, but only $\hat\rho$ is scale-free. $\hat\rho=\pm 1$ iff all points are collinear with the corresponding slope sign.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

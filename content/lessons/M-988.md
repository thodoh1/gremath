# Lesson M-988 — Stability intuition for numerical ODEs

Part X. X. Numerical Analysis · **Numerical integration & ODEs** · Medium

This lesson isolates **Stability intuition for numerical ODEs** and trains it until it is automatic.

## 1. Why this skill exists

Trapezoid, Simpson, error orders, Euler $y_{n+1}=y_n+hf(y_n)$, Runge–Kutta as higher-order idea, stability of stepping.

## 3. Precise statement

Absolute stability for $y'=\lambda y$: a one-step method $y_{n+1}=R(h\lambda)y_n$ is absolutely stable when $|R(z)|\le 1$ for $z=h\lambda$. Euler: $R(z)=1+z$, disk $|1+z|\le 1$. Implicit Euler: $R(z)=1/(1-z)$, stable on $\mathrm{Re}\,z\le 1$ actually the exterior of a disk — in particular the whole left half-plane (A-stable). Stiff problems (fast decaying modes) force tiny $h$ on explicit Euler but not on implicit Euler. Stability $\neq$ accuracy.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Euler on $y'=-20y$ with $h=0.1$: $R=1-2=-1$, $|R|=1$, on the boundary. With $h=0.2$: $R=1-4=-3$, unstable oscillations.

**Solution.** $|1+h\lambda|=|1-4|=3>1$, so the recurrence grows though the true solution decays.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** Implicit Euler with the same $h=0.2$, $\lambda=-20$: $R=1/(1+4)=1/5$, so

**Solution.** it decays, as the ODE does.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Taking $h$ large on a stiff explicit integrator because ‘the plot looks smooth on a coarse grid’ — it may be unstable garbage.
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** Stability region of explicit Euler in the $z=h\lambda$ plane.

**Solution.** The disk $|z+1|\le 1$.

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** A-stability means the left half-plane $\mathrm{Re}\,z\le 0$ lies in

**Solution.** the region $|R(z)|\le 1$.

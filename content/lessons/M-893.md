# Lesson M-893 — t distribution

Part U. U. Mathematical Statistics · **Inference** · Medium

This lesson isolates **t distribution** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Statistics is lighter than probability but bias, MLE, and tests do appear.

## 3. Precise statement

If $Z\sim N(0,1)$ independent of $V\sim\chi^2_\nu$, then $T=Z/\sqrt{V/\nu}$ has Student’s $t$ distribution with $\nu$ degrees of freedom. For iid $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$, $T=(\bar X-\mu)/(s/\sqrt{n})\sim t_{n-1}$. Tails are heavier than normal; as $\nu\to\infty$, $t_\nu\to N(0,1)$. Use $t$ when $\sigma$ is estimated by $s$ and data are approximately normal.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** $n=9$, $\bar x=2$, $s=3$, $H_0:\mu=0$. Observed $t$.

**Solution.** $t=2/(3/3)=2$ on $8$ df.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

- Using $z_{0.025}=1.96$ for $n=8$ unknown $\sigma$, or using $n$ df instead of $n-1$.

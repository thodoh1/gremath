## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Diagnostic six-pack: (1) $d/dx[x^x]$ at $x=1$. (2) $\int_0^\infty e^{-x}\,dx$. (3) Eigenvalues of $\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\ 0&3\end{pmatrix}$. (4) $30$-$60$-$90$ opposite $60^\circ$ if hypotenuse $10$. (5) $P(A\cup B)$ if $P(A)=P(B)=1/2$, $P(A\cap B)=1/6$. (6) $\lim n^{1/n}$.

**Solution.** (1) $1$ because $x^x(\ln x+1)$ at $1$ is $1$. (2) $1$. (3) $2,3$. (4) $5\sqrt{3}$. (5) $5/6$. (6) $1$. Gate: $\ge 5/6$ plus a trap note on each (power vs exponential; improper integral at $\infty$; triangular eigenvalues; opposite-side mix-up; inclusion-exclusion; log of the limit).

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

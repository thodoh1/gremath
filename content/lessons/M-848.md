# Lesson M-848 — Conditional expectation

Part T. T. Probability · **Joint distributions** · High

This lesson isolates **Conditional expectation** and trains it until it is automatic.

## 3. Precise statement

Conditional expectation $\mathbb{E}[X\mid Y=y]$ is the mean of the conditional law of $X$ given $Y=y$. As a random variable, $\mathbb{E}[X\mid Y]$ is a function of $Y$. Tower property: $\mathbb{E}\bigl[\mathbb{E}[X\mid Y]\bigr]=\mathbb{E}[X]$. If independent, $\mathbb{E}[X\mid Y]=\mathbb{E}[X]$.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** From $P(X=1\mid Y=0)=3/4$, $P(X=1\mid Y=1)=4/7$ with $X\in\{0,1\}$, find $\mathbb{E}[X\mid Y=0]$.

**Solution.** $3/4$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** $Y\sim\mathrm{Unif}(0,1)$ and $X\mid Y=y\sim\mathrm{Unif}(0,y)$. $\mathbb{E}[X\mid Y]$ and $\mathbb{E}[X]$.

**Solution.** $\mathbb{E}[X\mid Y]=Y/2$. Tower: $\mathbb{E}[X]=\mathbb{E}[Y/2]=1/4$.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Treating $\mathbb{E}[X\mid Y]$ as a number rather than a random variable, or replacing it by $\mathbb{E}[X]/\mathbb{E}[Y]$.
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

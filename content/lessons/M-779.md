# Lesson M-779 — Characteristic roots for recurrences

Part R. R. Combinatorics · **Advanced counting tools** · Medium

This lesson isolates **Characteristic roots for recurrences** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Combinatorics is additional topics and also probability setup.

## 3. Precise statement

Characteristic equation of $a_n=c_1 a_{n-1}+\cdots+c_d a_{n-d}$ is $r^d=c_1 r^{d-1}+\cdots+c_d$. Distinct roots $r_i$ give $a_n=\sum A_i r_i^n$. A root of multiplicity $m$ contributes $(A_0+A_1 n+\cdots+A_{m-1}n^{m-1})r^n$. Constants $A_i$ are fixed by initial conditions.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** $a_n=6a_{n-1}-8a_{n-2}$, $a_0=3$, $a_1=10$. Closed form?

**Solution.** $r^2-6r+8=0$, $(r-2)(r-4)=0$. $a_n=A\cdot 2^n+B\cdot 4^n$. $A+B=3$, $2A+4B=10\Rightarrow A+2B=5$, so $B=2$, $A=1$. Thus $a_n=2^n+2\cdot 4^n$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** $a_n=4a_{n-1}-4a_{n-2}$ has characteristic root

**Solution.** $r=2$ doubled: $a_n=(A+Bn)2^n$.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Forgetting the $n^j$ factors for a repeated root, or using $A r^n$ when two different roots are present but fitting only one initial condition.
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** $a_n=5a_{n-1}-6a_{n-2}$, $a_0=1$, $a_1=5$. Find $a_n$.

**Solution.** Roots $2,3$: $A+B=1$, $2A+3B=5\Rightarrow A=-2$, $B=3$. Thus $a_n=-2\cdot 2^n+3\cdot 3^n=-2^{n+1}+3^{n+1}$.

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** Check that formula at $n=2$.

**Solution.** $a_2=5\cdot 5-6\cdot 1=19$. Formula: $-8+27=19$.

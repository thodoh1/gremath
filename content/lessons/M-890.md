# Lesson M-890 — Type I and Type II errors

Part U. U. Mathematical Statistics · **Inference** · Medium

Prerequisites: `U.1-U.2`.


This lesson isolates **Type I and Type II errors** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Statistics is lighter than probability but bias, MLE, and tests do appear.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Type I and Type II errors**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

Type I error: reject $H_0$ when $H_0$ is true; $P(\text{Type I})=\alpha$ for a level-$\alpha$ test (simple $H_0$). Type II error: fail to reject $H_0$ when $H_1$ is true; $\beta(\theta)=P(\text{Type II at }\theta)$, power $=1-\beta(\theta)$. Decreasing $\alpha$ typically increases $\beta$ for a fixed sample size and test family.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

# Lesson M-417 — Fourier-series basics

Part J. J. Differential Equations · **Transforms & expansions** · Medium

Prerequisites: `J.1-G.3`.


This lesson isolates **Fourier-series basics** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. ODEs appear as additional or calculus-adjacent items: separable, linear first order, characteristic equations.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Fourier-series basics**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 2. From zero

### Landscape of *Transforms & expansions*

## 3. Precise statement

On $(-\pi,\pi)$, $f\sim a_0/2+\sum_{n=1}^\infty(a_n\cos nx+b_n\sin nx)$ with $a_n=\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^\pi f\cos nx\,dx$, $b_n=\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^\pi f\sin nx\,dx$. Even $f$ is cosine-only; odd is sine-only. At a jump, the series converges to the average of left and right limits.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Fourier sine coefficients of $f(x)=x$ on $(-\pi,\pi)$.

**Solution.** $f$ odd, $a_n=0$, $b_n=2(-1)^{n+1}/n$. Series $2(\sin x-\frac12\sin 2x+\frac13\sin 3x-\cdots)$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** The Fourier series of a $2\pi$-periodic square wave $\pm 1$ involves

**Solution.** only odd sine (or cosine, depending on phase) terms, amplitudes $\sim 1/n$.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Integrating $a_n$ against $\sin$ for an even function and expecting nonzero $b_n$.
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

**Solution.** $a_0=2$, constant term $1$.

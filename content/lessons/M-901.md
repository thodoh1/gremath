# Lesson M-901 — R-squared

Part U. U. Mathematical Statistics · **Regression** · Medium

Prerequisites: `K.8-U.1`.


This lesson isolates **R-squared** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Statistics is lighter than probability but bias, MLE, and tests do appear.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **R-squared**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

$R^2=1-\mathrm{SSE}/\mathrm{SST}$ with $\mathrm{SST}=\sum(y_i-\bar y)^2$ and $\mathrm{SSE}=\sum(y_i-\hat y_i)^2$, for a model with intercept. Equivalently $R^2$ is the square of the sample correlation between $y_i$ and $\hat y_i$ (and between $x$ and $y$ in simple linear regression). $R^2\in[0,1]$ here; $R^2=1$ means perfect linear fit of the observed points.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

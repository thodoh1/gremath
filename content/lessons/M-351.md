# Lesson M-351 — Double integrals

Part H. H. Multivariable Calculus · **Multiple integration** · High

Prerequisites: `H.1-E.3`.


This lesson isolates **Double integrals** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a High skill: it is standard GRE material. You should be able to do it without warming up. Multivariable calculus is explicitly in the calculus portion: partials, gradients, multiple integrals, Jacobians.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Double integrals**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

A double integral $\iint_D f\,dA$ is the limit of double Riemann sums, equal to signed volume under $z=f$ over $D\subset\mathbb{R}^2$. If $f=1$, it is the area of $D$. Fubini (next) computes it as an iterated integral.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** $\iint_{[0,1]\times[0,1]} 1\,dA$.

**Solution.** $1$ (area of the unit square).

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

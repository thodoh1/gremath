# Lesson M-915 — Orthocenter

Part V. V. Geometry · **Euclidean geometry** · High

Prerequisites: `C.1`.


This lesson isolates **Orthocenter** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a High skill: it is standard GRE material. You should be able to do it without warming up. Geometry is additional topics plus analytic geometry inside calculus/algebra items.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Orthocenter**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

The orthocenter $H$ is the intersection of the altitudes. In an acute triangle $H$ is inside; in a right triangle $H$ is the right-angled vertex; in an obtuse triangle $H$ is outside. Euler line: $H$, $G$, $O$ are collinear and $\overrightarrow{HG}=2\overrightarrow{GO}$ (so $HG=2\,GO$ and $G$ divides $HO$ in $2:1$).

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

- Putting $H$ at the centroid, or reversing the $2:1$ Euler ratio (that $2:1$ is also the median ratio for $G$, which is consistent because $G$ trisects $HO$).

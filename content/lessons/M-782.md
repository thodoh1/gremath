# Lesson M-782 — Vertices and edges

Part S. S. Graph Theory & Algorithms · **Graph basics** · Medium

Prerequisites: `Q.4-R.1`.


This lesson isolates **Vertices and edges** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Graph theory is additional topics: trees, Euler, handshaking, planarity.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Vertices and edges**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

Vertices $V(G)$ and edges $E(G)$ are the two types of objects in a graph. The order is $|V|$, the size is $|E|$. An edge is incident to its two endpoints; those vertices are adjacent (neighbors). Isolated vertices still count in $|V|$.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** A path on $6$ vertices. $|V|$ and $|E|$?

**Solution.** $|V|=6$, $|E|=5$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** A cycle $C_8$ has how many vertices and edges?

**Solution.** $8$ and $8$.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Forgetting isolated vertices when listing $V$, or counting each undirected edge twice in $|E|$.
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** Graph with $9$ vertices all isolated. Size?

**Solution.** $|E|=0$, order $9$.

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** $K_{2,3}$ has how many vertices and edges?

**Solution.** $5$ vertices, $6$ edges.

## 9. Lock it in

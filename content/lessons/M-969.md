Part X. X. Numerical Analysis · **Numerical error** · Medium

This lesson isolates **Truncation error** and trains it until it is automatic.

## 3. Precise statement

Truncation (discretization) error is the error from replacing an infinite or continuous object by a finite algorithm: cutting a series, replacing $f'$ by a difference quotient, replacing an integral by a quadrature rule. It would remain even in exact arithmetic. Taylor’s theorem is the usual source of truncation-error expansions in powers of a step $h$.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

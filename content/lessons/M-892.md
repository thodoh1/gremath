## 3. Precise statement

Normal-based $z$ procedures use $\bar X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2/n)$ exactly under iid normal data with known $\sigma$, or approximately by CLT for large $n$. Two-sample $z$ (independent, known $\sigma_i$): $(\bar X-\bar Y-(\mu_X-\mu_Y))/\sqrt{\sigma_X^2/n_X+\sigma_Y^2/n_Y}\approx N(0,1)$. Paired data reduce to a one-sample test on differences.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** $H_0:\mu=50$, $n=36$, $\bar x=53$, $\sigma=9$. Compute $z$.

**Solution.** $z=(53-50)/(9/6)=2$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

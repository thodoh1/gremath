# Lesson M-994 — Analyze extrema vs parameter

Part Y. Y. Mixed GRE Problem-Solving · **Parameter problems** · Critical

Prerequisites: `A.4-E.5-K.7-G.2`.


This lesson isolates **Analyze extrema vs parameter** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Critical skill: it is used constantly, and a hole here leaks points all over the paper. This is not content weight; it is how every weighted item is actually scored under a clock.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Analyze extrema vs parameter**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 2. From zero

## 3. Precise statement

For $f_t(x)$ on a domain, critical points solve $\partial_x f=0$, which may depend on $t$. Classify by $f_{xx}$ or a sign chart. Endpoints of a closed interval must be checked and may overtake interior critical values at a parameter threshold. Envelope/discriminant: the set of $t$ for which the min equals $0$ is often the interesting GRE cut.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** For $f(x)=x^3-tx$ on $\mathbb{R}$, find local max/min in terms of $t$.

**Solution.** $f'=3x^2-t$. If $t\le 0$, $f'\ge 0$, no interior extrema (strictly increasing if $t<0$). If $t>0$, local max at $x=-\sqrt{t/3}$, local min at $+\sqrt{t/3}$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** On $[0,1]$, $f(x)=x^2-tx$: the min is at $x=t/2$ if $t\in(0,2)$, else an endpoint. For $t=3$, min is

**Solution.** $f(1)=1-3=-2$.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Reporting only $f'=0$ and ignoring that for some $t$ that critical point leaves the interval.
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** $g(x)=x+t/x$ for $x>0$: min value as a function of $t>0$.

**Solution.** $2\sqrt{t}$ at $x=\sqrt{t}$ (AM-GM).

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** If a local min value is $4-t$, the graph of $y=f_t$ meets $y=0$ for which $t$?

**Solution.** $t\ge 4$ if that min is the global min and $f\to\infty$ at the ends.

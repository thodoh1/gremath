# Lesson M-891 — p-values

Part U. U. Mathematical Statistics · **Inference** · Medium

This lesson isolates **p-values** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Statistics is lighter than probability but bias, MLE, and tests do appear.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **p-values**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

The $p$-value is the smallest $\alpha$ at which the observed sample would reject $H_0$, equivalently $P(\text{test statistic as or more extreme than observed}\mid H_0)$ for a well-ordered test. Small $p$ is evidence against $H_0$. The $p$-value is not $P(H_0\mid\text{data})$ and is not the posterior probability of a mistake.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Two-sided $z$-test, observed $z=1.96$. Approximate $p$.

**Solution.** $2(1-\Phi(1.96))\approx 0.05$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

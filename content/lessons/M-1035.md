## 3. Precise statement

Gate — error log has no repeated concept misses: in the last two full sittings plus the last four timed $10$-item blocks, no concept appears as a miss more than once. ‘Repeated’ means the same M-skill (e.g. M-893 $t$ vs $z$, M-1005 extraneous roots, M-920 inscribed vs central) not the same surface story.

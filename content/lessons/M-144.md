# Lesson M-144 — Law of sines

Part C. C. Trigonometry & Conics · **Triangle trigonometry** · High

Prerequisites: `C.1`.


This lesson isolates **Law of sines** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a High skill: it is standard GRE material. You should be able to do it without warming up. Trigonometry appears in calculus, geometry, and as standalone identity/equation items. Exact values and identities are speed.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Law of sines**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 2. From zero

### Landscape of *Triangle trigonometry*

Law of sines, law of cosines, area $\frac12 ab\sin C$, Heron, and the ambiguous SSA case are the computational triangle toolkit. SSA can yield $0,1,$ or $2$ triangles; that is a GRE favorite. Inscribed-angle facts connect this topic to circles.

## 3. Precise statement

Law of sines: $\dfrac{a}{\sin A}=\dfrac{b}{\sin B}=\dfrac{c}{\sin C}=2R$ (circumdiameter). Use when you have a side and its opposite angle.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** In $\triangle ABC$, $A=30^\circ$, $B=45^\circ$, $a=10$. Find $b$.

**Solution.** $\dfrac{10}{\sin 30}=\dfrac{b}{\sin 45}$, $b=10\sqrt{2}$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** If $A=B$, then $a=b$.

**Solution.** Yes, isosceles.

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** Solve for $C$ if $A=40^\circ$, $B=60^\circ$.

**Solution.** $C=80^\circ$.

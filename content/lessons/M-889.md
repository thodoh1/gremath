# Lesson M-889 — Hypothesis testing

Part U. U. Mathematical Statistics · **Inference** · Medium

Prerequisites: `U.1-U.2`.


This lesson isolates **Hypothesis testing** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Statistics is lighter than probability but bias, MLE, and tests do appear.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Hypothesis testing**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

A hypothesis test of $H_0:\theta\in\Theta_0$ vs $H_1:\theta\in\Theta_1$ uses a rejection region. For a simple $H_0$, significance level $\alpha=P(\text{reject }H_0\mid H_0\text{ true})$. You either reject or fail to reject $H_0$; failing to reject is not a proof that $H_0$ is true. Typical $z$-test: reject $H_0:\mu=\mu_0$ ($\sigma$ known) if $|\bar X-\mu_0|>(\sigma/\sqrt{n})z_{\alpha/2}$.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** $H_0:\mu=0$ vs $H_1:\mu\neq 0$, $\sigma=1$, $n=9$, $\bar x=0.8$, $\alpha=0.05$, $z_{0.025}=1.96$. Reject?

**Solution.** $z=0.8/(1/3)=2.4>1.96$, reject $H_0$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** If you do not reject $H_0$, you have

**Solution.** insufficient evidence against $H_0$ at that $\alpha$, not a verification of $H_0$.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Accepting $H_0$ as proved, or two-tailed vs one-tailed mismatch with the scientific claim.
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** Critical value for one-sided $z$-test $\alpha=0.05$.

**Solution.** $z_{0.05}\approx 1.645$.

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** Same data, smaller $\alpha$: rejection becomes

## 3. Precise statement

Surface area of revolution about the $x$-axis: $S=\int_a^b 2\pi f(x)\sqrt{1+[f'(x)]^2}\,dx$ for $f\ge 0$. About the $y$-axis: $2\pi x\,ds$. The factor $2\pi r$ is the circle traced by a point at distance $r$ from the axis.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Sphere: revolve $y=\sqrt{r^2-x^2}$ on $[-r,r]$.

**Solution.** $ds=\frac{r}{y}dx$, so $2\pi\int y\cdot\frac{r}{y}\,dx=4\pi r^2$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** Cone: revolve $y=(R/H)x$ on $[0,H]$ about $x$-axis

**Solution.** $S=\pi R\ell$ where $\ell=\sqrt{H^2+R^2}$ is slant height.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** Revolve $y=x$ on $[0,1]$ about $x$-axis: $S$.

**Solution.** $2\pi\int_0^1 x\sqrt{2}\,dx=\pi\sqrt{2}$.

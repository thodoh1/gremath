# Lesson M-922 — Sector area

Part V. V. Geometry · **Circle & polygon geometry** · High

Prerequisites: `V.1-C.5`.


This lesson isolates **Sector area** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a High skill: it is standard GRE material. You should be able to do it without warming up. Geometry is additional topics plus analytic geometry inside calculus/algebra items.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Sector area**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

A sector of radius $r$ and radian angle $\theta$ has area $\frac12 r^2\theta$, or $\pi r^2\cdot(\theta^\circ/360)$. A segment (sector minus triangular portion) for $\theta\le\pi$ has area $\frac12 r^2(\theta-\sin\theta)$. Sector area scales with $r^2$; arc length with $r$.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Radius $4$, $\theta=\pi/2$. Sector area?

**Solution.** $\frac12\cdot 16\cdot\pi/2=4\pi$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Using $\frac12 r\theta$ (arc formula) for area, or mixing degrees into $\frac12 r^2\theta$.
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** Sector $60^\circ$, $r=6$: area.

**Solution.** $\frac12\cdot 36\cdot\pi/3=6\pi$.

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** Segment of a $60^\circ$ sector of radius $r$: area

**Solution.** $\frac12 r^2\bigl(\pi/3-\sqrt{3}/2\bigr)$.

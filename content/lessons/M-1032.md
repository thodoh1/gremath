## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Diagnostic eight-pack (definition $+$ number): (1) unbiased $s^2$ for $1,3,5$. (2) $95\%$ $z$-interval: $n=16$, $\bar x=0$, $\sigma=4$ (use $1.96$). (3) Res$(1/(z^2-1),1)$. (4) one Newton step for $x^2-2$ at $1$. (5) trapezoid $n=1$ on $\int_0^1 x^2$. (6) inradius of $3$-$4$-$5$. (7) $\chi^2_4$ mean. (8) Laurent inner radius of $e^{1/z}$ at $0$.

**Solution.** (1) $4$. (2) $\pm 1.96$. (3) $1/2$. (4) $3/2$. (5) $1/2$. (6) $1$. (7) $4$. (8) $0$ (essential, punctured plane). Gate: $\ge 6/8$ with a correct one-line definition beside each number.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

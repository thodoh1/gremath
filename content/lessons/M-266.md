# Lesson M-266 — Reduction-formula awareness

Part F. F. Calculus II — Integration · **Antiderivatives** · Critical

Prerequisites: `E.3-A.3`.


This lesson isolates **Reduction-formula awareness** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Critical skill: it is used constantly, and a hole here leaks points all over the paper. Integrals, FTC, and applications are first-class GRE citizens. Improper integrals feed series.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Reduction-formula awareness**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 2. From zero

### Landscape of *Antiderivatives*

An antiderivative of $f$ is $F$ with $F'=f$. The toolkit: power rule, substitution, parts, partial fractions, trig integrals, trig sub, reduction. Choosing the tool is the skill; carrying out the algebra is the tax.

### This skill, specifically

An antiderivative of $f$ is $F$ with $F'=f$.

You are not learning the whole topic again. You are learning the exact move named in the title. Neighboring lessons exist; use them if a word here is still foreign, then come back.

## 3. Precise statement

A reduction formula expresses $I_n=\int\sin^n x\,dx$ (or similar) in terms of $I_{n-2}$ via parts. Awareness: you need not derive every reduction on the GRE, but you should recognize $I_n=\frac{n-1}{n}I_{n-2}$ for Wallis integrals on $[0,\pi/2]$.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 4. How a working mathematician uses it

Write the hypotheses, apply the move, check the conclusion against the question asked.

On the exam, this should feel like a reflex: see the pattern, apply the move, check hypotheses, box the quantity asked for.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Wallis: $\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^n x\,dx$ for even $n=2$ equals

**Solution.** $\frac12\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}=\pi/4$. Formula $\frac{1\cdot 3\cdots(n-1)}{2\cdot 4\cdots n}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}$ for even $n$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 6. GRE-style problem

**Problem.** Reduction for $\int x^n e^x\,dx$

**Solution.** parts: $x^n e^x-n\int x^{n-1}e^x$.

Notice what was *not* done: no extra theory, no calculator, no essay. The governing idea, then the computation.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Memorizing the formula for the wrong interval (not $[0,\pi/2]$).
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** $\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^3 x\,dx$ by reduction/odd method.

**Solution.** $2/3$.

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

**Drill 2.** Why reduction exists.

**Solution.** Parts lowers the exponent by 2 for $\sin^n$.

## 9. Lock it in

You own this skill when you can do Drill 1 and Drill 2 tomorrow, mixed among unrelated problems, in under five minutes each, with the traps named in Section 7 avoided on purpose.

# Lesson M-980 — Interpolation error intuition

Part X. X. Numerical Analysis · **Interpolation & approximation** · Medium

This lesson isolates **Interpolation error intuition** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Medium skill: it appears less often, but when it appears it is often a discriminator. Numerical methods are additional topics: Newton, error, quadrature recognition.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Interpolation error intuition**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

If $f$ is $C^{n+1}$ and $p$ interpolates $f$ at $n+1$ distinct nodes, then $f(x)-p(x)=\dfrac{f^{(n+1)}(\xi_x)}{(n+1)!}\prod_{i=0}^n (x-x_i)$ for some $\xi_x$ in the interval spanned by $x$ and the nodes. The product $\omega(x)=\prod(x-x_i)$ is smaller (in max norm on $[-1,1]$) for Chebyshev nodes than for equal spacing, which is why Chebyshev interpolation avoids Runge growth.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Linear interpolant of $f$ at $a,b$: error formula?

**Solution.** $f(x)-p(x)=\dfrac{f''(\xi)}{2}(x-a)(x-b)$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 7. Traps that cost points

- Using $(n)!$ instead of $(n+1)!$, or thinking equal spacing minimizes $\|\omega\|_\infty$.
- Skip checking a candidate in the original equation.

## 8. Drills

Work these closed-book. Then check.

**Drill 1.** Interpolate $e^x$ at $0$ and $1$ by a line. Error bound on $[0,1]$ using $|f''|\le e$.

**Solution.** $|(x)(x-1)/2|\cdot e\le e/8$.

Cover that solution, redo the drill on paper, then continue.

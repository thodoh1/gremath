## 3. Precise statement

A median joins a vertex to the midpoint of the opposite side; each median splits the triangle into two equal areas. An altitude is perpendicular from a vertex to the line of the opposite side. An angle bisector splits an angle into two equal angles and, by the bisector theorem, divides the opposite side in the ratio of the adjacent sides: $\dfrac{BD}{DC}=\dfrac{AB}{AC}$.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** In $\triangle ABC$, $AB=6$, $AC=10$, $D$ on $BC$ with $AD$ the angle bisector, $BD=3$. Find $DC$.

**Solution.** $BD/DC=6/10=3/5$, so $3/DC=3/5$, $DC=5$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Sample unseen set (write answers, $25$ min): (1) $\mathrm{Cov}(X,X)$ if $\mathrm{Var}(X)=4$. (2) Incenter of a right triangle is not on the hypotenuse — true? (3) $\int_{|z|=1} z^{-2}\,dz$. (4) Euler step $h=1$ on $y'=-y$, $y(0)=1$. (5) Number of real roots of $e^x=x$. (6) $\mathrm{rank}$ of $uv^\top$ for nonzero $u,v\in\mathbb{R}^n$. (7) Median of $1,2,100$. (8) $R^2$ if $\mathrm{SSE}=0$. (9) Distance from $(0,0,0)$ to $x+y+z=3$. (10) Secant of $f(x)=x^2-2$ from $1,2$.

**Solution.** (1) $4$. (2) True (incenter is inside). (3) $0$. (4) $y_1=0$. (5) $0$ (graphically $e^x$ and $x$ never meet; $e^x>0$ and min of $e^x-x$ is positive). (6) $1$. (7) $2$. (8) $1$. (9) $\sqrt{3}$. (10) $4/3$. Gate: $\ge 9/10$ on this style of unlabeled mixed set, three times.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

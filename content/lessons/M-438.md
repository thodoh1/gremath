# Lesson M-438 — Gaussian elimination

Part K. K. Linear Algebra · **Systems & row reduction** · Critical

This lesson isolates **Gaussian elimination** and trains it until it is automatic.

This is a Critical skill: it is used constantly, and a hole here leaks points all over the paper. Linear algebra is a large share of the algebra portion. Rank, kernels, eigenvalues, and determinants are exam staples.

## 1. Why this skill exists

If you cannot do **Gaussian elimination**, every later item that secretly uses it becomes a two-minute panic. The GRE will not name the skill. It will drop you into a computation or a “which statement is true?” and wait.

## 3. Precise statement

Gaussian elimination uses elementary row operations to triangularize an augmented matrix $[A|\mathbf{b}]$, then back-substitutes. Each pivot column corresponds to a leading variable. The method is equivalent to left-multiplication by elementary matrices.

Copy that statement onto paper and replace each symbol with a tiny example. A definition you cannot instantiate is a definition you do not have.

## 5. Worked example

**Problem.** Solve $x+y=3$, $2x+3y=8$ by elimination.

**Solution.** $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$ gives $y=2$, then $x=1$.

Now cover the solution and redo it. If you cannot, you are still in Section 2.

- Adding a multiple of a column to another column (column ops change the unknown's meaning unless tracked).
